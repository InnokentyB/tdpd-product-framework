# Business Model

This module validates the sustainable value exchange before solution delivery.

Map user, buyer, budget owner, approver, procurement, legal/security reviewer, administrator, operator, champion, blocker, and beneficiary. Define user value, buyer value, funding or revenue mechanism, pricing/billing basis, delivery/support model, manual work, partner and contractual constraints, capacity, and scale limits.

Test willingness to pay or internal budget commitment through costly behavior where practical. Model unit economics with sourced, observed, assumed, and unknown values; use ranges and sensitivity rather than false precision. Include acquisition/adoption, implementation, infrastructure, vendor, compliance, sales, support, and currently unpaid labor.

Record `COM-DEC-###` as Proceed, Proceed with constraints, Revise, or Stop, including cost boundaries, capacity, residual uncertainty, and kill criteria.

See `templates/buyer-map.md`, `business-model.md`, `pricing-experiment.md`, `unit-economics.md`, and `commercial-decision.md`.
