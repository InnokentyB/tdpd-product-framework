# Business Model and Go-to-Market Compatibility Index

This compatibility entry preserves the pre-layered path. The authoritative modules are now independent:

The end-to-end commercial invariant remains:

`User value → Buyer value → Willingness to pay → Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Sustainable delivery`

The shared commercial decision remains `Proceed, Proceed with constraints, Revise, or Stop`; its rationale and evidence belong in the versioned handoff contract.

- [BUSINESS.md](BUSINESS.md) — buyer, value exchange, pricing/budget, unit economics, capacity, and commercial decision.
- [GTM.md](GTM.md) — positioning, channels, sales/decision motion, onboarding, activation, and market readiness.

New integrations should read the layer manifests in `core/layers/` and depend only on the contract they consume.
