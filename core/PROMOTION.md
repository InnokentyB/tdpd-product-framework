# Promotion and discovery

At Opportunity, ask how the intended audience already looks for help with
its job and how it could reach first value. Record a `PROM-###` hypothesis for
a new product, material audience expansion, or a change to an acquisition
route. For an internal tool, assess adoption through existing work surfaces and
owners. Record applicability and rationale for changes that leave these intact.

## Opportunity: choose a reachable job

1. Link the job to its actor, triggering situation, desired outcome, current
   workaround and source evidence. Distinguish user, buyer and approver.
2. Record market and language independently. Verify where the product can
   actually be delivered, supported and paid for. Check source language,
   local terminology and search results for the intended audience.
3. Record search intent and query or question variants in the audience's words.
   Cluster by the job and expected answer (learn, compare, evaluate, or start).
   Mark unvalidated phrases and unavailable data explicitly. Record source,
   date, market and measurement limits. Site impressions measure observed
   visibility for that site. Qualified interest and commitment need separate evidence.
4. Compare a primary and alternative route. Consider organic search (SEO),
   regional/local channels, AI search and citations, partnerships, directories,
   communities, events, newsletters, sales, referrals, and bounded paid tests.
   Select by observed behavior, offer fit, time, cost and measurement feasibility.
   Geography governs audience and delivery; AI-search visibility is a channel.
5. Bind the proposed material to a supported claim and its evidence, a feasible
   call to action and a first-value outcome. Set budget, owner, observation
   window, thresholds and continue/revise/stop rules in the linked experiment.

## GTM: connect content, product and measurement

Use the same hypothesis IDs in the channel plan, content brief, readiness
record and measurement contract. Every planned asset identifies its audience,
job, intent, locale, actual promise, supporting evidence and next action.
Reuse a page across related queries when it answers the same intent. Validate
technical discovery for the chosen channels, including crawl/index directives,
canonical and localized alternatives where applicable. Editorial review verifies
the answer, localization and claims. Planned or unavailable functionality needs
explicit wording and a feasible next action.

Prove the intended route from an entry page, campaign or internal invitation
to a persisted qualified action or first value. Check event capture, permitted
attribution properties and an error or refusal path where material. Use staged
or isolated fixtures when verification would otherwise spend money, send a
message or modify live records. Search rankings and AI citations are subsequent
observations. A technical test can verify page behavior and instrumentation.

## Outcome: review the measured route

Report visibility, qualified interest, commitment and activation separately.
Compare with the predeclared window and thresholds and record source, locale,
attribution limits and unknowns. Distinguish dashboard signals from direct
observations of the user's job. Feed the continue/revise/stop decision back
to Opportunity or GTM, whichever assumption failed first.
