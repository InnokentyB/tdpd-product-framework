# Go-to-Market

This module validates how the accepted product reaches, converts, onboards, activates, and retains the intended audience.

Define the initial segment, positioning, offer, packaging, acquisition/distribution channel, observable sales or decision stages, contracting/procurement, billing/entitlements, onboarding, first verified value, activation, support, capacity, communication, and measurement.

Validate the chain `Reach → Qualified interest → Commitment → Onboarding → Activation`. Do not scale acquisition while activation, retention, contribution, or guardrails fail. Keep support-assisted activation and cross-channel attribution limitations visible.

## Promotion strategy

In Product & Business, draft promotion hypotheses alongside the product bet. Start from an evidenced job to be done, its triggering situation, intended segment, and the language people use to describe the work. Carry the hypotheses into Launch & Operations and validate the selected channels before scaling.

Map each priority job to observed questions or query clusters, intent, target language and market, a page or content asset, and the next product action. Record evidence from interviews, customer messages, query data, or search results. Label unverified clusters as hypotheses. Include problem discovery, comparison, switching, and action intents where they occur. Use the same map to brief landing pages, guides, comparisons, case studies, and content for distribution.

Compare search SEO, generative search visibility (GEO), and geography-specific promotion where the market warrants it. Location-specific work may include localized language, offers and pages, local directories, communities, and partners. Compare against other routes where the audience already gathers or buys: outbound, newsletters, communities, partner channels, marketplaces, referrals, and paid search. Rank options by evidence of reach, qualified intent, cost, time to signal, and team capacity. Select a small set of initial tests and record why.

For selected SEO/GEO channels, verify crawlability, indexability, internal links, useful original evidence, and a clear path from the content to the product. Track qualified visits or leads and activation alongside impressions. Sample generative visibility only where observable, with a fixed query set, market, and date. Treat mentions as diagnostic and record attribution limitations. [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

The GTM gate requires the promotion strategy, selected experiments and owned content briefs (or an evidenced decision to defer content for the chosen channel), along with owners, instrumentation, commercial readiness, capacity boundaries, and pause/rollback/kill triggers before Launch.
