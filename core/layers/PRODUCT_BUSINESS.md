# Product & Business Layer

## Mission

Decide whether to invest, for which user and buyer, through which value exchange, and within what commercial and strategic boundaries.

## Owned modules

- `core/CONTEXT.md`
- `core/OPPORTUNITY.md`
- `core/BUSINESS.md`

## Gates

Context → Problem → Opportunity → Business

## Entry contract

A business question, available sources, decision owner, and known constraints. Product code or a finished specification is not required.

## Exit contract

- source-grounded product problem;
- opportunity and alternatives;
- ranked assumptions and experiment evidence;
- human opportunity decision;
- buyer/value exchange and commercial model;
- economic ranges, capacity, accepted uncertainty, and kill criteria;
- human commercial decision;
- handoff to Design & Requirements with explicit scope and non-goals.

## Independent uses

Opportunity audit, business-model review, pricing test, portfolio bet, pre-build validation, or stop decision.
