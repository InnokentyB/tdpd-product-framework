# Launch & Operations Layer

## Mission

Reach and activate the intended audience, operate the product safely, measure live outcomes and economics, and decide whether to iterate, scale, hold, roll back, or sunset.

## Owned modules

- `core/GTM.md`
- `core/OUTCOMES.md`

## Gates

GTM → Launch → Outcome

## Entry contract

Accepted product/UAT evidence, buyer and commercial boundaries, measurement and instrumentation contracts, rollout constraints, known risks, support model, and rollback path.

## Exit contract

- GTM readiness and activation path;
- controlled launch evidence;
- production outcome, guardrail, economics, support, and reliability observations;
- attribution limitations;
- human lifecycle decision;
- operational or sunset obligations;
- learning/change request routed to the earliest affected layer.

## Independent uses

Launch readiness review, migration/rollout, operating-model audit, adoption recovery, metric review, scaling decision, rollback, or sunset planning.
