## Metric classification

- **Primary outcome:** target user or business condition.
- **Input/leading:** behavior the team can influence.
- **Adoption/activation:** exposure and intended workflow entry.
- **Guardrail:** quality, cost, safety, compliance, reliability, or support constraint.
- **Operational health:** system and process sustainability.

Record definition versions. Do not compare periods using materially different formulas without an explicit reconciliation.
